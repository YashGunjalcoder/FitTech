Division one · Gym software

Your gym is good.
Your paperwork
is costing you.

Members expire without you noticing. Payments live in a register. New people search online and never find you. One website plus one member system fixes all three, and you run it from your phone.

People search, you don't appear
Someone looks up a gym in your area and walks into whoever shows up first. Without a site, that is never you.
You learn about an expiry too late
By the time you notice a member stopped paying, they have already decided to leave. A reminder three days early keeps them.
The register loses what the register holds
Who paid, which plan, when it ends. One spilled chai and a year of records is gone.
Agency quotes are built for corporates
City agencies quote a gym the way they quote a company, then take two months. We know what a gym actually needs and skip the rest.

You get both, not one or the other

Every gym we work with gets the public website and the member management system together. The website brings people in. The system keeps them paying. Separating them never made sense to us.

The public website

Mobile first and fast. Your equipment, your plans, your timings, your location on the map, and a WhatsApp button people actually press. Hosting handled on our side.

Member management

Add members, set their plan, record payments, mark them active or inactive. Opens in the browser on your phone or laptop. Nothing to install.

Renewal reminders

The dashboard shows who expires in the next few days. One tap sends them a WhatsApp message. This is the feature that pays for the whole thing.

Payment history

Every payment stored against the member. Who paid, how much, which plan, which month. Your monthly collection total on one screen.

Plans split the way you think

Cardio and non-cardio counted separately, because that is how you price and how you staff. Filter the member list by plan in one tap.

Division two · Student lab

Build your final
year project.
With someone
who has shipped.

For CS, IT and AI/ML students who want to come out of final year with a project they can defend in a viva, explain in an interview, and put on a portfolio. You write the code. We make sure it works, and that you understand it.

How this works, clearly

We are a mentorship lab, not a project shop. We help you choose a topic, design the architecture, review your code, unblock you when you are stuck, and prepare you for the viva. We do not hand over finished projects for you to submit as your own, and we will say no if that is what you are after. The students who get the most out of this are the ones who want to actually learn it.

Topic selection

A topic that is genuinely doable in your timeline, matches your syllabus requirements, and is interesting enough that a viva panel engages with it instead of nodding through.

Architecture & code review

Weekly sessions on your actual repository. Structure, data flow, model choice, where it will break. The feedback a good senior would give you if you had one.

Debugging support

Stuck for three days on an error nobody around you can read? Send it across. We find it with you and explain why it happened, so the next one takes an hour.

Report & documentation coaching

How to structure the report, what each chapter needs, how to present results and diagrams properly. We teach you the format and review your drafts. You write it.

Viva & demo preparation

A mock viva where we ask what the panel will ask. Most projects lose marks not on the build but on the student freezing when questioned about it.

Resume & portfolio

A resume that gets past screening, a GitHub that is readable, and a portfolio site that shows your work. Built properly once, used for every application you make.
